## Where a crest can come from

- D1/D2/D3 programs: the school athletics site (Sidearm) carries the logo; the importer already reads those pages for rosters.
- Club and box leagues: GameSheet club pages and league sites publish club logos. 
- PLL/MCLA/NLL: league site or Wikipedia infobox.

Once a logo lands in `public/logos/` and `team.logo` is set, the neutral tile disappears on the next build; nothing else needs changing.
